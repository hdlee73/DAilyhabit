THE RITE OF PENANCE 가톨릭 기도서

  • THE RITE OF PENANCE

    THE RITE OF PENANCE

    고해자를 맞이함
    The Reception of the Penitent

    ● In the name of the Father, and of the Son,
    and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

    ╬ May God, who has shone his light in our hearts
    grant that you may truly know your sins
    and his mercy.
    ● Amen.

    죄의 고백과 보속의 수락
    The Confession of Sins and the Acceptance of Satisfaction

    죄의 고백을 들은 다음 아래의 양식을 참조하여 훈계한다.

    ╬ Please make every effort to avoid the occasions of sin, live in the presence of the Lord and I'm sure you will live in the peace that only Jesus can give us.

    보속 예시
    Satisfaction(Acts of Penance)
    1. Say three Our Fathers and three Hail Marys.
    2. Recite the Rosary(5 Decades).
    3. Try to read Scripture more often, ideally on a daily basis, and endeavor to put into action the inspiration you gain from the Bible.
    4. Try to be more loving and kind toward your parents(husband/wife).

    고백자의 기도와 사제의 사죄
    The Prayer of the Penitent and the Absolution

    ● O my God,
    I am sorry and repent with all my heart
    for all the wrong I have done
    and for the good I have failed to do,
    because by sinning I have offended you,
    who are all good and worthy to be loved above all things.
    I firmly resolve, with the help of your grace,
    to do penance,
    to sin no more,
    and to avoid the occasions of sin.
    Through the merits of the Passion of our Savior Jesus Christ,
    Lord, have mercy.

    사제는 고해자의 머리 위에 두 손을 얹거나 적어도 오른손을 펴 들고 사죄경을 외운다.

    ╬ God, the Father of mercies,
    through the Death and Resurrection of his Son
    has reconciled the world to himself
    and poured out the Holy Spirit for the forgiveness of sins;
    through the ministryof the Church
    may God grant you pardon and peace.
    AND I ABSOLVE YOU FROM YOUR SINS,
    IN THE NAME OF THE FATHER,
    AND OF THE SON, ☩
    AND OF THE HOLY SPIRIT.
    ● Amen.

    하느님 찬미의 환호와 고해자 파견
    The Proclamation of Praise of God and the Dismissal of the Penitent

    ╬ Give thanks to the Lord for he is good.
    ● For his mercy endures for ever.

    ╬ The Lord has forgiven your sins.
    Go in peace.
